Target: emulator-5580, Android 12 x86_64 + ARM64 bridge.

SELinux stayed Enforcing throughout; no setenforce 0 and no new permissive domain.
The stock userdebug policy already marks su permissive; remote_control is enforcing.

Observed failure:

- Kernel audit:
  [17226.084668] avc: denied { read } comm="remote-control"

  tclass=file permissive=0
  [17226.091358] second identical media_variant_prop denial.

Verified policy/source cause:
- Live policy remote_control had coredomain/domain/netdomain, but no Codec2/OMX
  client grants: no media_variant_prop/codec2_config_prop reads, no codec
  hwservice find, no bidirectional Binder/FD access to codec services.
- shell belongs to appdomain. AOSP platform policy includes non-isolated
  appdomain in hal_codec2_client and hal_omx_client, explaining the difference.
- AOSP public/hal_codec2.te and public/hal_omx.te provide complete client grants.
- AOSP MediaCodecList.cpp queries media.player; MediaCodec.cpp queries
  media.resource_manager. Both service_contexts entries use mediaserver_service.
- Resource manager invokes client Binder callbacks (reclaimResource).
- MediaMetricsItem.cpp queries media.metrics; its getService retries if denied.

Source fix:
  hal_client_domain(remote_control, hal_codec2)
  hal_client_domain(remote_control, hal_omx)
  allow remote_control mediaserver_service:service_manager find;
  binder_call(remote_control, mediaserver)
  binder_call(mediaserver, remote_control)
  get_prop(remote_control, media_config_prop)
  allow remote_control mediametrics_service:service_manager find;
  binder_call(remote_control, mediametrics)

The policy uses AOSP macros and targets named media services; it does not make
remote_control an appdomain or grant access to arbitrary services/properties.
